# Rent Collection & Management System

A web-based rent management application designed to simplify how landlords manage rental properties, track tenant payments, and monitor outstanding rent.

The platform provides landlords with tools to manage rent collection, record partial and cash payments, monitor overdue billing periods, and notify tenants about outstanding payments. Tenants can view their rental information, track payment obligations, make payments, and access their rental history.

## Core Features

### Landlord
- **Dashboard:** Overview of collected rent, outstanding balances, and overdue payments.
- **Payment Management:** Record cash payments and track tenant transactions, including partial payments.
- **Rent Tracking:** Monitor billing periods and payment statuses.
- **Tenant Notifications:** Send notifications to tenants about overdue rent.
- **Property Management:** Manage rental properties and tenant lease information.

### Tenant
- **Dashboard:** View outstanding rent and current payment status.
- **Rent Payments:** Pay outstanding rent through the checkout process.
- **Payment History:** Review previous rent payments and their statuses.
- **Rental Information:** View current property details, landlord contact information, and billing periods.

## Key User Journeys

1. **Landlord Records a Partial Payment** – A landlord identifies an outstanding balance, records a cash payment, and sees the updated balance and payment status.
2. **Tenant Pays Rent** – A tenant selects an outstanding payment, proceeds to checkout, completes payment, and sees the updated payment record.
3. **Landlord Sends an Overdue Notification** – A landlord identifies an overdue tenant and sends a notification about the outstanding rent.
4. **Tenant Reviews Rental Information** – A tenant views their current rental property, landlord contact details, billing periods, outstanding balances, and rental history.

## Project Goals

- Simplify rent collection and payment tracking.
- Improve transparency between landlords and tenants.
- Support partial payments and accurate outstanding balance calculations.
- Reduce manual payment tracking and missed rent reminders.
- Maintain accessible rental and payment history.

## Current Development Focus

Building a reliable foundation for rental management, billing period tracking, payment allocation, and landlord-tenant workflows.
